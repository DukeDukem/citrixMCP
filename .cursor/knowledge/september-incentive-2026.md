## Booking plan for 500 € (32–34 points)

### Ideal (full Kat. 3)

1. **≥ 140 productive hours** in September  
2. **Cancel ≤ 5%** of booked shifts  
3. **Mondays (+ post-holiday) 08:00–10:00:** ≥ **7h** → with 4 Mondays (7./14./21./28. Sep 2026): book **both hours every Monday morning** (4×2 = 8 booked)  
4. **Same days 16:00–20:00:** ≥ **15h** → **full 4h every Monday afternoon** (4×4 = 16)  
5. **Tue–Fri 16:00–20:00:** ≥ **60h** (~3.5h effective in that window on most Tue–Fri days)  
6. **Saturdays:** ≥ **16h** → **4h every Saturday** (4×4)

### yoummday example combination (**33 points** → still Stufe 1 / **500 €**)

| Area | Plan | Points |
|------|------|--------|
| Hours | ~6h/day × ~22–23 days → ~135–138 productive (Kat. 2) | **7** |
| Mon mornings 08–10 | 2h × 4 Mondays → ≥7 productive | **6** |
| Mon afternoons 16–20 | 4h × 4 → ≥15 productive | **6** |
| Tue–Fri 16–20 | ≥60 productive | **4** |
| Absagequote | &lt; 5% (e.g. ≤10 cancels / 240 slots) | **6** |
| Saturday | 4h × 4 Saturdays = 16h | **4** |
| **Total** | | **33** |

To push **140h / 8 pts** (34 total): add a few hours beyond the 6h/day pattern.

### Practical weekly pattern

| When | Book |
|------|------|
| **Mon** | 08:00–10:00 **and** 16:00–20:00 |
| **Tue–Fri** | Heavy in **16:00–20:00** (toward 60h slot total) |
| **Sat** | **4h** each Saturday |
| **Any day** | Enough volume for ≥140h (or ≥120h if accepting 7 pts on hours) |
| **Always** | Show up; keep cancels very low |

## Treue-Bonus (separate from September points)

- From **April** onward (ongoing)  
- Per year of **active** worktype affiliation: **+0,01 € / minute** on base pay  
- Cap: **+0,03 € / minute** (3 years)  
- Not part of the 32-point / 500 € table

## What does *not* alone get 500 €

Quality/sales messaging and Treue-Bonus are separate. September **Produktivitätsbonus** is almost entirely: **hours + demand windows + low cancel rate**.
